02 / Services

Restore
the detail.

The customer-facing detailing layer remains simple. Every service begins with inspection and ends with documented handover.

Surface

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing that removes swirl marks, holograms and light scratches to bring back true gloss and depth.

Exterior

Deep Clean

pH-neutral foam wash, iron and tar decontamination, clay treatment and a streak-free hand-dried finish.

Interior

Interior Restoration

Steam cleaning, shampoo, leather conditioning, dashboard care and odour reduction for a clean cabin.

Engine

Engine Bay

Controlled degreasing and dressing of accessible surfaces without compromising sensitive components.

Wheels

Alloy & Glass

Wheel and caliper cleaning, tyre dressing, glass polishing and hydrophobic finishing.

Lighting

Headlight Restoration

Hazy lenses are corrected, polished and sealed for a cleaner front end and sharper light output.
